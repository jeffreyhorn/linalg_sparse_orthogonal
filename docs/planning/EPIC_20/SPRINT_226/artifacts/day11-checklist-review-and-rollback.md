## Proposed rollback triggers and recovery sequence

**Owner:** rollback owner **unassigned**; release maintainer must name the person and approve the destination-specific steps before publication. These steps are a proposed procedure, not an executed rollback or authority to publish.

1. **Trigger before publication:** stop if the selected SHA, tag target, archive hash/manifest, installed package, version/width, license contents, first-use result, required CI gate, or approved claim text differs from the signed-off packet. Also stop on a failed/missing required job or absent owner signoff. Record a no-go with the failing evidence and keep all assets unpublished.
2. **Trigger after publication:** if a consumer-visible installed-package regression, wrong-width artifact, incorrect source/license payload, bad tag/asset identity, unsupported claim, or security/legal defect is confirmed, the rollback owner opens an incident record keyed to the immutable tag, source SHA, asset name and original SHA-256. The release maintainer and relevant platform/API/license owner assess scope.
3. **Contain:** halt any in-progress upload, disable advertised download links and further distribution where the chosen destination permits, and preserve the original asset and logs for audit. If a GitHub release is the approved destination, the owner must determine its actual release/asset controls then; no release or tag is present now. Mirrors and prior downloads cannot be erased.
4. **Notify and correct:** use the approved communications channel to state affected versions/platforms, impact, workaround and status. Make a new reviewed candidate, rerun affected archive/install/quality/hosted/first-use gates, and publish a corrective *new* version and immutable tag only after a new go decision. Do not move the old tag or replace bytes silently.
5. **Close:** verify links and notices point to the corrective record, retain both original and replacement hashes, source manifests, CI receipts, approvals and incident timeline, then record what changed in the checklist. The rollback owner and communications channel remain to be approved; until then this is a blocking plan, not a ready recovery capability.
