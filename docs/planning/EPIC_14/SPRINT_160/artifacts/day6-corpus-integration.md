# Day 6 Fixture And Corpus Integration

## Summary

Day 6 tied the new `qr-compatible-ls` generated comparison family to
source-controlled report metadata and selected comparison freshness policy.

The selected fixture `qr_overdetermined_compatible_5x3` remains maintained by
the source-controlled dense reference helper and existing QR solve proof owner,
not by the generated corpus fixture manifest.

| `tests/test_normalize_report_index.py` | Updated focused fixtures and expectations for the 12-row selected comparison set. | Missing, duplicate, stale, fail, defer, and unexpected behavior must stay covered after the second family is added. |

external-library parity, raw QR basis identity, Q sign/orientation, global
rank-threshold policy, broad rank-deficient solve, hosted CI proof, platform
portability, package-manager support, shared-library ABI, performance
superiority, and state-of-the-art status.

## Selected Comparison Freshness Policy

The selected comparison set now contains 12 rows:

- six `qr_underdetermined_minnorm_2x4` rows under
  `build/comparison/qr_minnorm/study.tsv`;
- six `qr_overdetermined_compatible_5x3` rows under
  `build/comparison/qr_compatible_ls/study.tsv`.

`make report-index-comparison-freshness` regenerates both targets before
running strict freshness normalization. This prevents stale local artifacts
from creating accidental pass evidence.
