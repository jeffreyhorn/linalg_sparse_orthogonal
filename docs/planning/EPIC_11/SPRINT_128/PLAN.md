# Sprint 128 Plan: QR Residual Claim-Gate Closure & Corpus Semantics

**Sprint Duration:** 14 days
**Goal:** Convert Sprint 127's remaining compatible/wide residual, wide
nullspace, threshold-family, SuiteSparse corpus, optional-large, exact
minimum-norm, and helper-movement deferred debt into bounded evidence or explicit
future-owner decisions before Q/economy, partial-SVD, corpus-index, performance,
package, and adoption work consume those truth boundaries.

**Starting Point:** Sprint 128 begins from:
- Sprint 127 residual deferred debt, final validation package, and non-claim
  register
- Sprint 125-127 rank-deficient QR metric, tolerance, threshold, corpus,
  optional-data, and minimum-norm gates
- current QR residual, nullspace/subspace, SuiteSparse, optional-large,
  minimum-norm, QR-vs-SVD, and helper-boundary evidence
- current `tests/test_qr.c`, `tests/test_qr_solve.c`,
  `tests/test_colamd.c`, `tests/qr_external_dense_reference.py`, SuiteSparse
  corpus fixtures, and QR/SVD helper ownership boundaries

The sprint must:
- map Sprint 127 carry-forward work without duplicating completed Sprint
  121-127 policy, fixture, or claim-gate work
- accept compatible zero-residual and wide residual-only QR evidence only when
  distinct trust value and underdetermined output semantics are explicit
- expand wide, near-threshold, and SuiteSparse nullspace/subspace evidence only
  after rank/nullity, projection metrics, tolerance, support tier, and
  sparse/economy output semantics are pinned
- add or explicitly defer dependent-row, wide, default-threshold, and
  SuiteSparse threshold families with expected ranks, diagnostics, support tier,
  and failure interpretation recorded before implementation
- gate SuiteSparse rank-deficient QR corpus evidence behind independent
  expected-rank metadata, threshold semantics, skip behavior, runtime budget,
  diagnostics, and validation requirements
- gate SuiteSparse and optional-large QR/minimum-norm evidence behind explicit
  extraction rules, shape/nnz/RHS metadata, rank/nullity claims, residual/norm
  metrics, missing-data skips, runtime/platform expectations, and support tier
- add QR-vs-SVD minimum-norm cross-checks, larger exact underdetermined lanes,
  and helper movement only as bounded behavior-specific evidence, not broad
  SVD-pseudoinverse, unique-basis, or external-library parity

**End State:** Sprint 128 leaves behind:
- Sprint 127 residual dedupe and dependency map
- compatible zero-residual and wide residual-only decision package
- wide/near-threshold nullspace and subspace evidence or explicit deferrals
- remaining QR threshold-family evidence or explicit deferrals
- SuiteSparse rank-deficient QR corpus gate
- SuiteSparse and optional-large minimum-norm decision package
- exact minimum-norm cross-check/helper movement gate and non-claim update

**Time budget:** Each day is capped at 12 hours as requested. This day-by-day
plan totals `166` hours, matching the Sprint 128 project-plan estimate.
