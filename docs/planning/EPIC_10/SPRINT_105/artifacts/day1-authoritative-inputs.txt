Sprint 104 handoff inputs
-------------------------

docs/planning/EPIC_10/SPRINT_104/artifacts/day14-closeout-and-handoff.md
  Hands Sprint 105 these constraints:
  - preserve benchmark/sentinel claim boundaries when touching reordering,
    graph, or large-matrix benchmark evidence;
  - treat local timing as command/fixture/backend/thread-context evidence,
    not portable performance proof;
  - keep performance-sentinel hard-fail behavior limited to S5 unless a
    future baseline design justifies additional thresholds;
  - check POSIX CMake registration and Windows expected CTest count explicitly
    when adding tests;
  - keep OpenMP ownership wording near new parallel regions or benchmark
    interpretation;
  - do not widen optional dense backend language without tests, docs, and
    platform-scope updates.

docs/planning/EPIC_10/SPRINT_104/RETROSPECTIVE.md
  Reinforces that generated benchmark metadata can drift into overclaiming,
  local sentinel expansion remains incomplete by design, Windows count
  discipline remains manual, and optional/OpenMP evidence remains bounded.
