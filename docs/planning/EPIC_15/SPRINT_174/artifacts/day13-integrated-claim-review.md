external-library parity. Reasonable follow-up candidates are:

   explicit fixture, helper contract, row set, and non-claim boundary.
2. Consider a separate LU CSR comparison lane only if it has its own
   source-controlled helper path and report-family row; do not infer it from
   linked-list LU.
3. If hosted promotion expands, preserve the current split between local
   generated artifacts and source-controlled proof-owner metadata.
4. Keep selected comparison freshness focused on the named artifacts; broad
   report-index freshness remains unselected.
5. Preserve package-manager, shared-library ABI, runtime-loader, performance,
   platform, release, and state-of-the-art non-claims unless a future sprint
   selects and validates those surfaces directly.
