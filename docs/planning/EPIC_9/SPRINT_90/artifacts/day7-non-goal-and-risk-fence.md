# Sprint 90 Day 7: Non-goal and Risk Fence

## Purpose

Fix the anti-sprawl contract for Epic 9 before the final review, todo, and
project-plan package is written, so later sprint planning cannot widen the
epic into fake product claims or undisciplined rewrite work.

## Main Result

Sprint 90 now has one explicit Epic 9 non-goal and risk fence:

- Epic 9 *will* pursue:
  - bounded compressed-first product-model convergence
  - bounded portable backend maturity
  - one real capability-breadth widening
  - bounded runtime/proof/product-coherence improvement
  - bounded build/package/workflow convergence
  - broader but still disciplined comparison depth
- Epic 9 *will not* pursue:
  - a blanket full-library compressed rewrite
  - fake platform symmetry
  - fake broad complex or mixed-precision maturity
  - fake benchmark supremacy
  - generic rewrite-without-proof modernization

## Explicit Non-goal Fence

The strongest invalid widenings are now fixed:

- no blanket "rewrite the whole library in compressed form" story
- no product claim that the linked-list shell disappears entirely
- no broad complex-scalar claim without real implemented and tested support
- no broad mixed-precision claim without real implemented and tested support
- no claim of symmetric Linux/macOS/Windows reviewed parity
- no claim of broad shared-library-first package maturity unless a later sprint
  truly lands and proves it
- no claim of universal best-in-class runtime, fill quality, or solver
  performance from bounded touched benchmark slices
- no "modernize everything" source/test/doc rewrite detached from the ranked
  contradiction map and maintained proof ownership

## Execution-Risk Map

The strongest execution risks are now explicit:

- branch-wide sprawl:
  - the target is ambitious enough that uncontrolled widening would collapse
    prioritization and make the review/todo/project-plan package incoherent
- build/package drift:
  - reducing duplication or widening backend/package lanes can easily outrun
    maintained install/export proof and reviewed parity if not tightly staged
- proof-owner overload:
  - the largest implementation and proof hotspots are also likely to be
    touched again, which raises regression and review-cost risk if batches get
    too wide
- runtime/benchmark overclaim:
  - bounded reorder/runtime evidence is useful, but it can be misread as broad
    product timing proof if the comparison contract is not kept explicit
- claim inflation from partial wins:
  - a real improvement on one lane can tempt the docs or plans to overstate
    maturity across untouched lanes

## Stable "Will Do / Will Not Do" Boundary

The stable execution boundary is now fixed:

### Epic 9 Will Do

- move the public product reading closer to compressed-first compute reality
- raise the backend ceiling with at least one bounded portable lane
- widen capability on at least one high-value real lane
- reduce the strongest remaining runtime/control, maintenance, chronology, and
  duplication costs where they materially affect product credibility
- broaden comparison evidence in a bounded, reviewable, claim-disciplined way

### Epic 9 Will Not Do

- pretend the repo becomes a fully generalized industrial sparse stack in one
  epic
- collapse bounded maintained proof into ad hoc benchmark or workflow claims
- reopen every historical rough edge at once
- treat every large file or every sprint-era comment as equally urgent
- trade truthfulness for ambition in packaging, backend, runtime, or
  capability wording

## Carry-Forward Boundary List

The strongest residual areas that may still remain explicit carry-forward work
after Epic 9 are now fixed:

- broader external correctness comparison beyond the bounded maintained lanes
- broader ecosystem runtime and reorder/fill comparison depth
- broad complex and mixed-precision maturity if Epic 9 only lands one bounded
  capability widening
- full symmetric cross-platform package and workflow maturity
- complete elimination of large mixed-role source and proof owners
- full removal of all historical chronology from every permanent surface

## Strongest Clarification

The useful Day 7 clarification is now explicit:

- Epic 9 should be ambitious, but only inside a hard truthfulness fence
- the epic is allowed to move the repo materially closer to a state-of-the-art
  sparse numerical product
- it is not allowed to do that by quietly broadening claims faster than proof,
  implementation, and ownership maturity

## Exit State

- Sprint 90 now has one stable anti-sprawl contract.
- The strongest invalid widenings, execution risks, and carry-forward boundary
  areas are explicit.
- Later review, todo, and project-plan writing can use one durable "will do /
  will not do" boundary instead of re-deciding scope ad hoc.
